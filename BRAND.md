# Doot — brand direction, round one

## Brand idea

**Doot (दूत)** is the trusted emissary for your inbox. Email carries messages; Doot does the work around them—finding, understanding, organizing, and preparing safe actions for approval.

The distinction is deliberate: *daak* is the mail, while *doot* is the actor handling it.

## Naming architecture

- Product: **Doot**
- Endorsement: **by Oddship**
- Full formal name: **Oddship Doot**
- Devanagari anchor: **दूत**, used sparingly in the mark and brand moments
- Category: local email emissary

## Positioning

For people with inboxes that have become working archives, Doot is a local email emissary that investigates the full cache, builds useful views, and prepares reviewable flows—without silently changing the mailbox.

## Voice

- Calm, precise, observant
- Proactive without presuming authority
- Technical without “AI magic” theatre
- Uses direct verbs: inspect, find, prepare, review, apply
- Says what changed and what did not

## Core lines

- Primary: **A trusted emissary for your inbox.**
- Functional: **Carries context. Prepares action. Waits for approval.**
- Oddship-flavoured: **Built locally. Kept on course.**

## Visual system

- Warm paper background from rohanverma.net for long-session readability
- Deep ocean ink and peacock cyan from Oddship for navigation and Doot actions
- Marigold for human attention, selection, and approval boundaries
- IBM Plex Mono–style system stack, thin rules, square corners, bracketed navigation
- The D-shaped messenger mark combines an envelope fold, forward motion, and a marigold dispatch dot
- The full `दूत` spelling remains a cultural anchor in considered brand moments, not inside the compact mark

## Guardrails

- No gradients, glossy AI purple, or chatbot sparkle overload
- Do not overuse Devanagari or nautical metaphors in functional UI
- Doot may prepare; the user approves mailbox changes
- Email content remains visually neutral so brand styling never damages readability
