# Code of Conduct

We want Doot to be a welcoming, constructive project. Treat contributors and users with respect, assume good intent, discuss ideas rather than people, and avoid harassment or discriminatory behavior.

Project maintainers may edit or remove harmful content and restrict participation when needed to protect the community. Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers through the contact options on the Oddship GitHub organization. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.
